# The Signal

Elias reached the lamp room out of breath. The light out on the water
blinked again — three pulses, then dark.

"You saw it too," said Mira. She was already at the window, glass in hand.

"Out past the rocks?"

"Past the rocks, and moving fast."

Elias took the glass from her and steadied it against the frame. For a
long moment neither of them spoke.

"It's a boat," he said finally. "Small one. No business being out in
this."

"Then we signal back," Mira said, and reached for the shutter lever.

"Wait." He caught her wrist. "If we answer, whoever's out there will
turn for the cove. The rocks will have them before we can light the
channel."

"And if we don't answer," she said quietly, "the sea will have them
anyway."

The old keeper looked at the lever for a long time. "Light the channel
first," growled the old man at last. "Then we answer."
